Meet Roam. Good places. Real stories. A little help getting outside.

Instead of losing a beautiful place in an endless feed, Roam puts the memory beside a map. Explore parks, cafés, trails, and cultural places. Open a story, save somewhere you like, and turn that inspiration into a visit.

In the local prototype, sharing starts with a photo and a short experience. Speak naturally, review the transcript, and confirm the place. Gemma helps fill the draft. Browser identity and a private recovery file keep your memories yours, without a signup wall.

The important part is grounding. Your original experience stays intact. Gemma selects facts from retrieved sources, and Roam attaches the actual source text. If a place has no matching background, the app says so. It does not invent opening hours, prices, or things you never experienced.

Want to go there? Choose how much time you have. Local Gemma builds a plan from cited memories, with a walking budget and clear labels when example stories are used.

Listen to a summary, stop playback whenever you want, and then put the phone away.

The website is live on Render, with Tiger Data connected. It is a read-only preview. The complete AI workflow runs locally with Gemma through Ollama. Private cloud photo storage and GPU moderation are still being connected, so automatic approval remains off.

Fifty-four automated tests pass. This walkthrough uses clearly labelled example memories and illustrative photos.

Roam. Less scrolling. More going.
